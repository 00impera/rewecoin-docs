1. What is ReweCoin?

ReweCoin (RWC) is an independent Proof-of-Work blockchain built on the Zcash codebase (zcashd 6.0.0). Mainnet launched on September 21, 2026 with no protocol-level premine, no founders' reward in the consensus rules and no genesis allocation. Public mining is open through the ReweCoin pool at pool.rewecoin.com.

It inherits Zcash's transaction model (transparent and shielded pools, UTXO ledger) and Equihash consensus, as a fully separate chain with its own genesis, emission schedule and parameters.

2. Why does it exist?

ReweCoin was created to build an independent blockchain ecosystem focused on real utility, digital assets and accessible Web3 services. The goal is a single platform that brings together wallets, NFTs, wrapped assets and decentralized applications, without depending entirely on other blockchain networks.

3. How does it work?

3.1 Consensus

Proof-of-Work with Equihash 200,9, memory-hard and designed to keep mining accessible.

  • Target block time: 75 s
  • Difficulty adjustment: dynamic, per block (inherited from zcashd)

3.2 Transaction model

Transparent and shielded pools (Sapling/Orchard present in the protocol; usage so far is mostly transparent). Mainnet addresses start with C7.

3.4 Mining pool (in testing)

  • Stratum server for Equihash 200,9, including NU5 block templates.
  • Local Equihash verification of every solution before it reaches the node.
  • Payout address required: miners connect as -u ADDRESS.worker; the address is validated by the node.
  • Found blocks are journaled; payouts wait for coinbase maturity (101 blocks), skip orphans, and are idempotent.
  • Not yet implemented: variable difficulty (vardiff), PPLNS share accounting, TLS.

4. What utility does it have?

Today RWC is base-layer infrastructure: send, receive, mine and explore on the public ledger, with a web wallet, a block explorer and a bridge to Monad. Wrapped ReweCoin (wRWC) extends RWC to the Monad ecosystem through a 1:1 bridge. A ReweCoin DEX interface is published at wallet.rewecoin.com/dex.html. No liquidity pools are active yet, and liquidity features are under development.

Directions for the ecosystem, which are plans and not commitments:

  • Vaults
  • NFT Packs and trading cards
  • wRWC integration in ReweCoin applications
  • Integration with DeFi services and Monad-compatible platforms, being evaluated as the ecosystem develops

5. Supply and emission

Detail: tokenomics/supply-and-emission

  • Initial block reward: 50 RWC
  • Halving interval: 840,960 blocks (~2 years)
  • Premine: none at protocol level (no genesis allocation, no founders' reward). Public mining is open through the ReweCoin pool at pool.rewecoin.com.
  • Asymptotic supply: ~84,093,434 RWC
  • Technical overflow cap: 100,000,000 RWC (not the target)

7. Risks and disclaimers

  • Early-stage network: difficulty is at its floor and peer count is minimal. Block timing and security assumptions will shift as the network grows.
  • Emission projections assume the target block time; real timelines vary with hashrate.
